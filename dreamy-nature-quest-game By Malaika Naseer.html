Play Online

Online co-op needs a Node.js + Socket.IO server, which isn’t part of this build yet. Offline play with your companion works fully.

How to play

WASD move · drag mouse to turn camera · Space jump · Shift run · E collect · H hint · Esc pause

Touch: left joystick moves, drag anywhere else to look, use the round buttons.

Each level has 3 rounds. Walk up to glowing items and collect them all. Your pink companion follows you, and Hint points you the way.
